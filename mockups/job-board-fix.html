What changes in code: the store methods stop swallowing the write result (apply, status update, archive) so the screen can tell saved from not-saved; the consent box becomes a real checkbox and the submit button stops being pre-disabled; the job form gains rate, schedule, location, duties and requirements so the customer card has something to show; the apply route refuses a second application and shows a not-found state instead of “Open Position”.
