Customer mobile buttons — press feedback

Today on a phone the Docufy buttons do nothing when tapped: every state the shared Button primitive has is written as a hover rule, and a touch device never hovers. Pick how a tap should feel. Press and hold each control — on a phone, or with a mouse.

Desktop today (hover) ← the lift + fill you already know

Nothing below reacts to hover — only to a press, so phone and desktop read the same.

1 · Mirror the hoverMost literal

While held, the button becomes exactly what it looks like under a mouse cursor: fills brand blue, lifts 2px, shadow. Releases back on lift-off.

2 · Hover fill + press-inRecommended

Same fill, but it squashes 3.5% instead of lifting — a lift hides under your fingertip, a squash reads as the screen taking your press. Settles back with a 180ms overshoot.

3 · Fill + rippleMost animated

Fills like option 1 and adds a ripple from where your finger landed, 380ms. The only one that clearly moves even on a full-card tap, but it is the least like the rest of Docufy.

Scope I would implement it in: one block in src/styles/tailwind.css behind @media (hover: none) and (pointer: coarse) — real touch devices only, so a narrow desktop window keeps the hover behaviour untouched — gated by an html attribute set only when the signed-in role is customer (plus the public Landing Page). That is 3 files (tailwind.css, Layout.tsx, LandingPage.tsx) and zero per-button edits, so it covers all 58 primitive + 82 raw buttons in the customer flow and everything inside their dialogs automatically. The app's existing reduced-motion rule already disables it for people who turn animations off.
